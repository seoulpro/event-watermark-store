# Code of Conduct

Treat other participants with patience and respect. Discuss observable
behavior, technical tradeoffs, and reproducible evidence rather than personal
characteristics.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and publication of private
information are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove disruptive
contributions and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project
safe and useful.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the security contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
